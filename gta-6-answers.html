GTA 6 Answers

One page, straight answers. I preordered on PS5 the day preorders opened, I am playing the minute it unlocks, and every answer here gets checked by hand before it goes up. No scraped wikis, no guessing. If I have not verified something yet, the card says so.

Launch week moves fast and this page fills in as I play. Bookmark it.

Before launch

When does GTA 6 come out?

Thursday, November 19, 2026, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Digital copies unlock at midnight, so West Coast players get in the night before. My copy unlocks Wednesday, November 18, at 9 PM Pacific.

Checked November 14, 2026

How much is GTA 6?

Standard is $79.99 and Ultimate is $99.99. The $150 price tag that floated around was never real. I broke down every version in the price post.

Checked November 14, 2026

Can I preload GTA 6?

Yes. Preload opened November 12 on PS5 and Xbox. It is a big download, reported around 212 GB with everything, so clear your space before launch night, not during it.

Checked November 14, 2026

Is GTA 6 coming to PC?

Not at launch. Rockstar has not announced a PC date, so console is the day-one way in. I preordered on PS5 for exactly that reason.

Checked November 14, 2026

Who are the main characters?

Lucia and Jason, a couple cutting through Leonida, Rockstar's Florida. The trailers show both of them playable.

Checked November 14, 2026

Who voices Lucia and Jason?

Rockstar has not announced the cast. Reporting points to Manni L. Perez for Lucia, but that is reported, not confirmed, and Jason's actor is still unconfirmed. This answer gets updated the day it is official.

Checked November 14, 2026

Is GTA Online in GTA 6 at launch?

Rockstar has not confirmed a new Online at launch. Until they say it themselves, treat every leaked Online detail as a rumor.

Checked November 14, 2026

Launch night and after

Gameplay answers, checked and dated

These come from Rockstar's own pages and published reviews, checked the day reviews landed and dated on every card. Nothing here comes from a trailer or a rumor. As I play through Leonida myself, my own checks get added the same way.

Checked November 18, 2026

What is the first thing to do in Leonida?

Fix your settings before anything else. Sprint moved to L3 in the new Default layout, so switch to the Classic scheme if you want the GTA 5 layout, and turn subtitles on, they ship off. Then work the Key Lento side content, the first chunk of the story lives there, and do not grind for money: the story drains your accounts in the first stretch, so early wealth is wasted effort.

Checked November 18, 2026

How does the wanted system work now?

Police track a description, not you personally: your face, your outfit, any visible weapon, your vehicle, and whether your partner is with you, with CCTV feeding sightings and the scanner broadcasting your vehicle and build. Every factor has a fix, change clothes, swap cars, holster the weapon, split up. Break line of sight and a heat level still hangs over the area for a while. The top end is six stars. And at one star, unarmed, you can hold LT to focus on the officer and talk the star away entirely.

Checked November 18, 2026

Where is the early money?

Underwater treasure hunts unlock very early and are the easiest clean money at the start. Before you even have a bank account, small jobs pay cash through your mailbox; one walkthrough puts $600 on a job called Lab Fees and $150 on Unwanted Attention. Robberies become the main income once they open up, and where you sell matters, because fences pay specialist prices for narcotics, jewellery, and marked bills. Your BuckMe bank account arrives in Chapter 1, at mission 12.

Checked November 18, 2026

What happens in the first hour?

The prologue is a flashback: Lucia's robbery ends in a chase you cannot win, and she starts the game in prison before an early release for overcrowding. Jason is in a Keys shack running jobs for Brian. The first real mission, Gone Fishing, ends at Jason's house in Key Lento, your first base. Chapter 1 runs 22 missions.

Checked November 18, 2026

Which languages does GTA 6 have full voice acting in?

English is the only one. PlayStation's official support page lists voice and text for English, and text only for French, German, Italian, Korean, Polish, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Simplified Chinese, both Spanish variants, and Traditional Chinese. One wrinkle worth knowing: the Xbox store lists 13 supported languages and Japanese appears as a screen language in some listings, but PlayStation's own table has 12 and no Japanese.

Checked November 18, 2026

What is GTA 6 rated for, exactly?

M for Mature 17+, and the ESRB's full descriptor list is Blood and Gore, Intense Violence, Mature Humor, Nudity, Strong Language, Strong Sexual Content, and Use of Drugs and Alcohol, plus an In-Game Purchases notice. The ESRB has published no longer written summary for the game, so any site quoting one is making it up.

Checked November 18, 2026

How many GB is GTA 6, really?

Rockstar's only official number is a ceiling: up to 212 GB depending on your device. The two numbers floating around, about 210 and about 225, are the same file measured two ways, gigabytes versus gibibytes. Anyone giving you one exact size without saying which unit they mean is guessing.

Checked November 18, 2026

Is there a 60 fps mode? What resolution does it actually run at?

There is no performance mode anywhere, every console targets 30 fps. Digital Foundry measured a native 1440p on PS5, PS5 Pro, and Series X, with the Pro upscaling to 4K through PSSR and getting ray-traced shadows the other consoles do not have. Series S renders at 720p internally and upscales to about 1080p. Expect dips into the 20s when the destruction gets heavy.

Checked November 18, 2026

Does GTA 6 have difficulty settings?

No difficulty levels. But buried in Settings, under Gameplay, options 24 and 25, sit two assists most players will never find: Accessible Combat, where you take less damage and deal more, and Reduced Vehicle Damage. Push Square confirms switching them on does not change the story and does not disable trophies.

Checked November 18, 2026

Can I get the GTA 5 controls back?

Yes. The Classic control scheme is modeled on GTA 5, alongside the new Default and a Left-Handed option. Aiming offers Lock-On, Partial, and Free Aim with an assist strength slider. Subtitles are off by default at first boot; the menu adds a Mission Only mode, speaker names, and captions, and UI text size runs from Small to X Large.

Checked November 18, 2026

How does saving work in GTA 6?

You get 15 manual save slots plus one separate autosave slot. There is no mission replay, no medal system, and no New Game Plus, and the story branches on your choices, so reviewers are advising a rotation: a fresh manual save roughly every 7 percent of story progress, so a decision you regret is never gone for good.

Checked November 18, 2026

What happens to my money if I get busted or wasted?

Cash in your wallet is gone. Money in your BuckMe account is safe. Push Square's tip is to treat ATMs as checkpoints: the BuckMe app carries an ATM Finder, and pressing Down shows both balances at once.

Checked November 18, 2026

How do I get my car back from the impound?

Through the Scooter Bros app, which spawns, repairs, and retrieves your owned vehicles from police impound for a fee. Scooter Bros Premium costs $12,500 and includes 3 free uses per in-game day, which pays for itself fast if you keep losing cars. For plain repairs, gas stations charge less than the app does.

Checked November 18, 2026

Can you buy houses in GTA 6?

No. Safehouses are handed to you by the story, one per chapter, and none of them are for sale. The property you can buy is garages, through the Vice City Real Estate app, and only once you have left Key Lento. Garages are also how you grow past your starting vehicle slots.

Checked November 18, 2026

How long is GTA 6's story?

Review consensus puts the main story at about 60 hours, around 80 hours with side content, and 300 hours or more for a full 100 percent run. The Guardian's reviewer needed about 70 hours to finish, and roughly 30 of those just to reach Vice City.

Checked November 18, 2026

Are there Strangers and Freaks in GTA 6?

Yes, under a new name: Optional Missions. The trophy asks for 8 completed strands, and here is the catch nobody warns you about: the game gives you no checklist for them. The only way to track your progress is watching the trophy counter before and after a strand's final quest.

Checked November 18, 2026

Does GTA 6 have a photo mode?

Yes, at launch, opened from the pause menu with Square on PS5 or X on Xbox. You get multiple aspect ratios, aperture and depth of field control, filters, and a three-point lighting rig: spot, flood, and softbox. The one limit IGN flagged is that you cannot pose Lucia or Jason, or change their expressions.

Checked November 18, 2026

Is there a day-one patch for GTA 6?

There is no official day-one patch and no patch notes, and that is the confirmed answer. Preloaded copies did get two updates before launch, one around a gigabyte on November 13 and a smaller one on November 16, 602.5 MB on PS5 and about 1.59 GB on Xbox. Rockstar has not said what either of them changes.
