GTA 6 launches in …

Confirmed launch facts

Everything here comes from Rockstar, Take-Two, PlayStation, Xbox or The Game Awards themselves, checked November 18, 2026. If they haven't announced it, it isn't on this page.

Release
Thursday, November 19, 2026. Rockstar Support says the game unlocks at midnight local time in all regions; US store listings point to a single midnight Eastern unlock, which is 9 PM Pacific on the 18th. No edition gets early access.
Platforms
PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S only. No PC version has been announced, and there is no disc: physical copies are a code in a box.
Editions
Standard is $79.99. Ultimate is $99.99, digital only, and the Ultimate Upgrade is also sold on its own for $20. Buying either edition before November 20 adds the Vintage Vice City Pack, and digital purchases before then also include a month of GTA+. (Take-Two's announcement)
Download
Pre-load opened November 10, and Rockstar says to clear up to 212 GB depending on your device. (Rockstar Newswire)
The story
Lucia Caminos and Jason Duval, in the state of Leonida. Confirmed places: Vice City, the Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga. Also on Rockstar's cast list: Cal Hampton, Boobie Ike, Dre'Quan Priest, Real Dimez, Raul Bautista and Brian Heder. (Only in Leonida)
Performance
Rockstar is targeting 30 frames per second on both consoles. On PS5 Pro it adds PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution and ray-traced shadows. No 60 fps mode has been announced.
Online
A single-player game at launch. Rockstar has announced no GTA 6 online mode, and the free GTA+ month in the launch offers is for the existing GTA Online.
Rating
ESRB Mature 17+.
Awards
Eight nominations at The Game Awards 2026, the most of any game, including Game of the Year, and Manni L. Perez is nominated for Best Performance. The ceremony is December 10. (The Game Awards)
