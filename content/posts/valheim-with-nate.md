## Update: Four-Man Crew &mdash; October 10, 2026

The [new update](https://www.valheim.com/news/) dragged us back in. For me Valheim&rsquo;s a placeholder until [GTA 6 drops November 19](gta-6-one-month-out.html). Pretty sure it&rsquo;s the same for Enjii. Probably the same for half the gaming world, honestly.

We started fresh. New world seed, blank slate&hellip; except we kept our characters from the last run. Enjii set us up on a paid server this time. Generous man. Loading in and seeing those empty trophy stones hit me: we&rsquo;re grinding all of this again. Well, sort of. The server&rsquo;s running 2x resources, and thank god for that, because there&rsquo;s no way we&rsquo;d finish in time otherwise.

{{< side-photo src="optimized/valheim-portals-800.webp" width="1206" height="678" alt="A row of glowing portals inside our Valheim base" caption="The portal row" >}}

I went north right away and threw up a little 4x4 shelter after farming wood around the spot I&rsquo;d picked for our first base. Enjii joined me almost immediately and we just&hellip; farmed. An hour later we had a massive base going. That first base is getting turned into a farm now. Enjii&rsquo;s got plans for it, now that we&rsquo;ve got the fortress up. That&rsquo;s the longhouse above and the furnaces below, by the way. The guys did work.

Bosses so far: the big ass deer. The Temu version of Grimer. And the dragon, Moder. Or as I call that fight, the jankiest battle we&rsquo;ve ever had, and somehow one of the most entertaining. I know I&rsquo;m forgetting one more boss but I can&rsquo;t care enough to try and remember.

{{< side-photo src="optimized/valheim-furnaces-800.webp" width="1206" height="678" alt="Blast furnaces glowing at night in our Valheim base" caption="Blast furnaces at the fortress" >}}

The dragon fight is my favorite so far, mostly because Xander was there. We herded that thing into landing on a structure the guys built and just unloaded obsidian arrows into it until it dropped. The trophy doesn&rsquo;t hang itself, you have to haul it back to spawn and mount it on the stone yourself. Thankfully we&rsquo;ve got like six portals up now, the row in the second picture, so getting around isn&rsquo;t the chore it used to be. [Enjii](https://linktr.ee/enjiimusic) climbed the whole damn mountain and claimed it. Stolen valor, I say. Haha.

We&rsquo;re a four-man Viking crew now. The builds are looking dope, all credit to the guys. Grinding materials is easier with help, and honestly the whole game is just more fun with a crew. Next biome soon. I&rsquo;ll keep better notes this time so I can actually relay it properly in the next update.
