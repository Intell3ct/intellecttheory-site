Back on a previous trip to the San Francisco Ferry Building&apos;s restaurant Gott&apos;s, I happened to look out the window while ingesting their amazing brussels sprouts and the special of the day lobster burger.

## The Trucks That Did It

And I saw three LED billboard trucks parked on the Embarcadero. Lo and behold, I see Pikachu &mdash; turns out those trucks were for [Pok&eacute;monXP](https://pokemonxp.com/en-us), the fan event running alongside the [2026 Pok&eacute;mon World Championships](https://worlds.pokemon.com/en-us) in San Francisco... that piqued my curiosity, because I hadn&apos;t opened the Pok&eacute;mon GO app since 2019.

{{< side-photo src="optimized/pogo-billboard-800.webp" width="1206" height="1608" alt="LED billboard truck advertising Pok&eacute;monXP with a spacesuit Pikachu" caption="The trucks that did it." >}}

## Back in 2019

That was the last time I played it with my childhood friend before he moved to Ohio. He used to drive around while we&apos;d catch them &mdash; I&apos;d hold both phones and throw the Pok&eacute; Balls, and we&apos;d get out of the car to do raids with other &ldquo;trainers.&rdquo;

## The Walk That Hijacked Dinner

Having the game open, I started spinning Pok&eacute;Stops in the vicinity of where I was eating, and if you know San Francisco, you know there&apos;s no lack of those. Ended up getting some stamps for the local event, and with that came the opportunity to catch a spacesuit Pikachu with a San Francisco background... and that right there sparked my rekindled addiction to this game. What turned out to be a ferry ride and a meal at the Embarcadero ferry station ended up getting hijacked by Pok&eacute;mon. I had a few family members with me, and I ended up &ldquo;gently&rdquo; suggesting we go for a walk after the meal to help with digestion. The reality was I wanted to go spin more Pok&eacute;Stops and catch more Pikachus. An hour of walking up the Embarcadero later, we returned to the ferry departure station and I had 150 Pok&eacute;mon in my account. Just like that, I was back in.

{{< side-photo src="optimized/pogo-pikachu-800.webp" width="1206" height="2622" alt="Event Pikachu in a space costume with the Golden Gate Bridge background" caption="The one that started it all." >}}

## A Month and a Half Later

At this point it&apos;s been about a month and a half since I started playing again. I&apos;ve gone to a couple of in-person raids with my community ambassador, got a couple of free gifts from them, some codes for ingame items, and I&apos;ve participated in a few Community Days. I&apos;ve even been purchasing the monthly pass for this. Anyways, I just wanted to share how I picked up this game again &mdash; and it&apos;s forced me to walk a little bit more. I&apos;ll [share more down the line](my-best-ones.html) on this game, as I have an active rivalry with some neighbors since I&apos;m Team Mystic and, well, they keep trying to take over my local gym.

Game on.
