I wrote this the day Brady passed and kept it in my personal journal &mdash; that&rsquo;s why it says September 24, 2024, even though this site just went live. He meant too much to me to not honor him here.
</aside>

Unfortunately, today was a devastating day. My best friend and special being left us for his final voyage. All dogs go to heaven. This was something I remember from the animated movie with Burt Reynolds with the same name. I choose to believe that.

Brady was the most special dog to me. The moment I saw his face on the post asking to re-home some puppies, I had a moment of clarity and certainty that he needed to be with us. A decision that, although with pain and sorrow now, I will never regret.

I picked him up and came home, only to drop him off with my mom so I could continue with my destructive habits of smoking and drinking. I regret this. Even through my stupid decision, I came home to find him waiting for me. I walked through the doors and he ran from the women adoring him to choose me. I am lucky.

As time went on he grew, and I grew away... away from my family and onto my own. Six years passed and I returned a different persona. Still one that loved me, but with less trust. I deserved it.

Through this time we all made amazing memories and experiences together with my good boy. I am glad for every single one of them.

He ran to me that day... out of everyone in that room, he picked the one who had just left him behind. That&apos;s the thing about dogs. They don&apos;t keep score. They don&apos;t hold your worst against you. They just love you, and wait for you to become someone worth loving back.

I don&apos;t know if all dogs go to heaven. But I know one did today. Rest easy, Brady. You were the best of us.
