Four days. That's all it took.

Rockstar dropped the GTA 6 launch trailer on October 15, and inside its first four days it passed 51 million views. On Rockstar's own channel alone. That's not counting reposts, clips, reactions, any of it. Just the one upload.

Here's the part that got me. The GTA V launch trailer, the one from 2013, sits at about 34 million views. Thirteen years of views. Different era, YouTube was a fraction of the size back then, I know. Still. GTA 6's trailer lapped it in four days, and the game isn't even out yet.

Here's the rundown. Over 20 million views in the first 16 hours, 51 million by day four per GTADB's count. For scale, most games would kill for those numbers across their entire lifespan, trailers, ads, everything combined. This was one video, less than a week. The hype is the marketing.

## Nobody Is Ready for November 19

I've been saying [Valheim is just a placeholder until GTA 6 drops](valheim-with-nate.html), and I mean it more every week. Half the gaming world is quietly clearing their November. And honestly? Good. It's been over a decade since GTA V. A whole console generation grew up on GTA Online. Of course the trailer lapped its predecessor in under a week. What did anyone expect?
