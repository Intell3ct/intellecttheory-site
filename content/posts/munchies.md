## Spicy Korean Chicken Feet (Dakbal)

This is the recipe I used, from [Seonkyoung Longest on YouTube](https://youtu.be/QZJMleA2NxU). Serves 4.

### What you need

**For the chicken feet:** 1 lb chicken feet, 2 Tbs Korean coarse sea salt, 1/2 cup soju (or vodka or milk) for cleaning.

**For the sauce:** 3 cloves garlic, 3 small chunks ginger, Thai chilies to taste (optional but do it), 1 Tbs gochujang, 2 Tbs gochugaru, 1 Tbs soy sauce, 1 Tbs mirin, 1 Tbs oyster sauce, 1 Tbs sugar, 1/4 tsp black pepper.

**To finish:** sesame oil, sesame seeds, chopped green onion.

### How it goes

Clean the chicken feet with the salt and soju, then steam them until tender. While that&apos;s going, blend the sauce ingredients together. Toss the steamed feet in the sauce, garnish with sesame oil, sesame seeds, and green onion. That&apos;s it.

Fair warning: the gochugaru brings real heat. Start with less if you&apos;re not sure, you can always add more.

Full step-by-step with pictures is on [her video](https://youtu.be/QZJMleA2NxU).
