Did you realize everyone is busy with their own lives and only reach out when it&rsquo;s either beneficial to them personally or to their social status perception? I mean really dwell on why you contact someone. It seems that no matter how benevolent you truly are, there is a sliver of selfishness attached to your actions&hellip; you can try and refute it but wouldn&rsquo;t that be your pride intervening? You so badly want to be seen, or even think of yourself as a selfless human, that you&rsquo;re doing the opposite.

## The Question Nobody Asks

Think about the last time you reached out to someone. Was it because you genuinely missed them&hellip; or because you missed the version of yourself that existed when they were around? Did you call to check on them&hellip; or to feel like the kind of person who checks on people?

## It Doesn&rsquo;t Make It Fake

That doesn&rsquo;t make your care fake. It makes it human.

Maybe true selflessness was never about having nothing to gain&hellip; maybe it&rsquo;s knowing exactly what you gain, and choosing to show up anyway.
