I Call It Evolution

July 4, 2026

The “pussyfication” of America? I blame the fathers who weren’t there — and I praise the mothers who raised those boys the best they could. Those mothers gave a different kind of love than a boy would have gotten with his father around.

We no longer live in times where you can disrespect a woman and get away with it, and I’m glad we can no longer inflict pain and despair on our amazing counterparts. We must love our women, protect them, and most of all respect them!

You wouldn’t be reading this if it were not for the woman who birthed you.
